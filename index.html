BOMBACLAAT

TACTICAL BOARD GAME

📖 MANUAL & RULES

Goal: Be the last player standing!

Turn System: Each player has 2 Action Points (AP) per turn.

  • 🏃 Move 1 tile = 1 AP
  • 💣 Place Bomb = 0 AP (Max 1 per turn)
  • ⏭️ End Turn early = 1 AP

Bombs & Explosions:

  • Bombs tick down for 2 turns and explode at the start of the 3rd turn.
  • Explosion shape: Cross (+) with radius of 2.
  • Center of explosion = Instant Kill!
  • Edge of explosion = -1 HP.

Items (Drop from Breakable Walls):

  • 🧨 Fire Up: Bomb radius +1
  • 💣 Bomb Up: Max bombs on board +1
  • 👟 Speed Up: Permanent +1 AP per turn

Controls:

Player 1 (Red)

Move: W, A, S, D

Bomb: B

Skip: V

Player 2 (Blue)

Move: Arrow Keys

Bomb: Enter

Skip: Shift
